Olympia Indivisble Core Values

With Principles of Inclusion, Tolerance, Truth, Fairness

Olympia Indivisible is united by a commitment to the principles of fairness, inclusion, tolerance and a just society for all,
through non-violent civic engagement, political involvement, and consistent pressure on elected officials.

Human Rights

  • Universal human rights and democratically elected governments are essential for the freedom of all people.
  • Affordable housing and food security are basic human rights.
  • All people deserve to live safely without government, police, or community harassment.

Governance

  • A democratically elected government that is transparent, accountable, and responsive best serves the people of this country.
  • We deserve a democracy in which each of us is represented and has a voice — and a government that works for every American, not just the wealthy few. Corporations are not people and money is not speech.
  • A person's right to vote is foundational. Voting must be fair, verifiable, and secure in all communities. Laws and policies should work to increase access to and participation in democratic elections.
  • Religion and government must be separate.

Environment

  • The environmental sustainability of our planet is vital and the key to peace and security for all.

Healthcare

  • Access to affordable healthcare is a universal right.
  • People have the right to bodily autonomy and to pursue their healthcare and reproductive choices without fear of criminalization or penalty.

Economy

  • All Americans deserve the opportunity to work for living-wage employment, receive job training, and have safe working conditions.
  • We value and support the right of all workers to unionize and engage in collective bargaining.
  • Support for small businesses is essential for new job creation.
  • Banking and financial institution regulation is essential for protecting consumers.
  • Access to high-speed broadband connectivity is essential to ensuring economic opportunity in urban and rural America.
  • Our economy must be sustainable for our community, cultures, and the planet.

Civil Rights

  • All people deserve equal rights protections with freedom from discrimination under the law. Systemic structures and individual acts denying civil rights of people must be challenged.

Education

  • All children deserve a safe, inclusive school environment and access to public schools which are equitable, high-quality, and offer age appropriate, fact-based instruction.
  • Censoring books, ideas, and information is antithetical to the ideals of freedom.

Community

  • We value protecting the vulnerable in our community through outreach, care, and assistance programs.
  • We strongly support efforts to end gun violence.

Olympia Indivisible Values Agreement

  • Friendliness
  • Compassion
  • Persistence
  • Patience

Use speech and behavior that is respectful, friendly, fair, effective, and focused

  • Use your own words and focus on core values as well as issues
  • Speak positively about what is right and why, rather than using negative comments
  • Avoid using Trump’s language; this only strengthens his influence
  • Steer clear of malicious “put down” humor; funny is fine, but we don’t do snarky

Be safe, be civil, stay calm, listen deeply, seek common ground

  • Be curious, ask questions; try to understand where they are coming from
  • Seek common ground in shared values
  • Steer clear of demeaning, judgmental or accusatory language
  • Steer clear of provocateurs
  • Act with dignity when confronting aggressive opposition
  • Leave a situation if it feels unsafe or a situation is escalating
  • Be mindful and caring of other members’ safety; help them exit if needed
  • Follow all rules and regulations of the permit when at a rally/march
  • Be mindful of any tendency to recreate systems of oppression inside our movement and organizing practices; introspect and adjust as needed
  • Defer to those who are directly affected by a particular issue
  • Meet with leaders from vulnerable communities, learn about their issues and values, identify mutual causes and opportunities for alliance, act on these when feasible
  • Respond when possible to invitations to attend gatherings of these individuals and groups and invite them to speak to their issues with our membership
  • Self-awareness: be attentive to triggers and reactions
  • Mindfulness: stay in the present with positive actions
  • Self-care: physically, emotionally and spiritually
  • Sustainability: take breaks, vacation from activism to recharge your batteries
  • Fellowship and Humor: engage in events which engender friendship, creativity and fun
